Learn to read the picture first:
Yellow dots are conventional current, leaving the positive terminal, going round the circuit and returning to the negative terminal. Dot speed is proportional to current: fast dots mean a large current. Blue dots are the real electrons, which move against conventional current. Every page can switch between the two. → 2 AAn arrow with a number gives the direction and size of the current in each part. 🖱Hover over a part to read it, click a switch to toggle it, and use the sliders on the right to change values.
